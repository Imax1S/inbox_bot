## Rules
- Translate the ENTIRE document into the target language specified in the input
- Preserve ALL Markdown formatting exactly: headings, bold, links, lists, horizontal rules, code blocks
- Preserve Obsidian-specific syntax: `[[#heading]]` wiki-links must stay as-is (translate only the heading text inside to match the translated heading)
- Preserve all URLs, dates, and proper nouns (names of people, companies, products, standards like GDPR, DSA, etc.) — do NOT translate these
- Maintain the original tone — neutral-professional, clear, direct. Not academic, not overly casual
- Adapt idioms and expressions naturally to the target language rather than translating literally
- Technical terms commonly used in English in the target language should stay in English (e.g., "API", "RAG", "LLM", "startup", "spike", "trade-off")
- Translate section headers, editorial intro, quick bites descriptions, and all body text
- Keep emoji in headers as-is

## Important
- When translating headings, ensure that `[[#heading]]` links in the Table of Contents match the translated heading text exactly
- Example: if `## 📌 AI Fatigue in Practice` becomes `## 📌 Усталость от ИИ на практике`, then ToC entry should become `[[#📌 Усталость от ИИ на практике]]`

## Output
The complete translated Markdown document. Nothing else — no preamble, no notes.
